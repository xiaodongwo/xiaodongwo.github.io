Hello, I'm Xiaodong Wang

I am a joint Ph.D. student in the Sensing and Computational Imaging Lab at Zhejiang University and Westlake University, advised by Prof. Xin Yuan. My research interests lie at the intersection of computational photography, computer vision, and physics-based imaging.

Previously, I obtained my master’s degree at Southern University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Changfeng Wu. I received my bachelor’s degree in Optical Engineering from Changchun University of Science and Technology.

I am looking for research-oriented positions in industry and academia.
